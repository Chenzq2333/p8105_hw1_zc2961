``` r
as.numeric(pull(p2_df, log_vec))
as.numeric(pull(p2_df, char_vec))
as.numeric(pull(p2_df, fac_vec))
```

- Logical variables are successfully coerced into numeric values(1 for
  True and 0 for False). R implicitly converts them to numbers and
  calculates the proportion of TRUE values.
- Character variables cannot be coerced into numbers and are converted
  to NAs, thus taking the mean of a character vector fails and returns
  NA.
- Factor variables are coerced into their underlying integer
  representations (e.g., 1, 2, 3 corresponding to their factor levels).
  However, taking the mean of the original factor directly still fails
  because R strictly treats factors as categorical data, preventing
  meaningless arithmetic operations unless explicitly coerced.
